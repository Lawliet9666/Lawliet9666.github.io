    <div class="biography-copy">
      <p>I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Oakland University.</p>
      <p>
        My research advances trustworthy robot autonomy by combining learning-based methods with formal guarantees from control theory. I am
        interested in safety-critical control, reinforcement learning, multi-robot task and motion planning, and generative models for robotics.
      </p>
