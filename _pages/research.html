--- layout: archive title: "Research" permalink: /research/ author_profile: true --- {% include base_path %} My research focuses on developing fundamental algorithms that enable large teams of autonomous robots to accomplish collaborative tasks intelligently in cluttered environments.

Multi-robot Coverage

We propose a spatial decomposition based coverage algorithm that mitigates the issue of local minima trapping in general unstructured obstacle environments supported by convergence guarantees.

Multi-robot Pursuit-evasion

We propose a novel pursuit strategy that utilizes the Voronoi partition method to collectively encircle the evader while ensuring collision avoidance, ultimately leading to its capture.

Multi-robot Search and Coverage for Infrausture Inspection

We propose a fully autonomous infrastructure inspection and information management system with advanced multiple unmanned aerial systems and AI technologies
